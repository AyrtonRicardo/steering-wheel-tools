Determine how many encoder counts fall within each degree of rotation — and verify your setup meets OpenFFBoard's recommended minimums for smooth force feedback.

Configuration

Check your encoder datasheet. Most quadrature encoders multiply this ×4 — enter the base PPR, not the quadrature count.

OpenFFBoard uses full ×4 quadrature decoding by default.

Set to 1 for direct-drive. For a 3:1 reduction pulley enter 3; the encoder sees 3 turns per wheel turn.
